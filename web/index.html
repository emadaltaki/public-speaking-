If Speaking Makes You Nervous, You're Not Alone.

My heart starts racing. I overthink everything I want to say. I'm afraid people will judge me. I know what I want to say, but I freeze. These feelings don't mean you're bad at speaking. They mean you're human.

How It Works

Show up to a welcoming session where everyone is learning. Speak in a supportive environment. Reflect on what worked and where you can improve. Grow your confidence through repetition and community.

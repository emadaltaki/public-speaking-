You're not alone

Speaking makes you nervous? Most of us feel the same.

  • “My heart starts racing.”
  • “I overthink everything I want to say.”
  • “I'm afraid people will judge me.”
  • “I know what I want to say, but I freeze.”

Feeling this way doesn't mean you're bad at speaking. It just means you care.

Good to know

Questions people ask

What if I'm really nervous?

Then you're in good company. Most people here felt the same way at first. We take it slow and practice together.

Do I have to speak in my first session?

Not if you don't want to. We'll tell you how a session works before you come, and you can start small. Nobody will push you.

How much does it cost?

One session is $50. You can also get 4 sessions for $180 or 8 for $320. Your first call is free.
